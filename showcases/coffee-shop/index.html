A place with a little personality

Come for a coffee.
Stay for the feeling.

In this imagined story, Ember & Grain begins with a neighbourhood-sized ambition: make the everyday coffee stop feel considered.

Warm materials. A place to settle. A menu that does not need explaining. The design brings those ideas together through photography, expressive type and clear, practical information.

This is original fictional brand copy—not the history of a trading café. Stock photographs depict real spaces elsewhere and do not imply affiliation.
