What a tradesperson’s website needs

Make it easy for customers to choose you

A good tradesperson’s website does not need to be complicated. It needs to clearly show what you do, demonstrate the quality of your work and make it simple for a customer to contact you.

Clear services

Show the work you offer in plain English, whether that is landscaping, plumbing, electrical work, decorating, roofing or another trade.

Real work examples

Project photos, before-and-after images and short descriptions help customers understand the standard of work they can expect.

Easy contact options

Make it easy for people to call, message on WhatsApp, complete an enquiry form or request a quote.

Trust signals

Reviews, qualifications, insurance information, service areas and clear business details can help a customer feel more confident.

Mobile-first design

Many customers will find you on a phone. Your website should be easy to read and use on a small screen.

A clear next step

Every page should make it obvious what the customer should do next, such as request a quote, call you or send a message.
