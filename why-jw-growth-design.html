Sources, scope and what the animation means

The numbers animate once to their published values. They are not a real-time data feed and are not JW client-performance results.

  • ITU: estimated worldwide internet users in 2025, not concurrent activity.
  • Ofcom: reported UK adult daily time online, not time spent buying or comparing businesses.
  • BrightLocal: a representative panel of 1,002 US adult consumers via SurveyMonkey. Do not extrapolate this percentage to the UK or the global internet population.
  • The globe and journey do not display or consume user locations, purchase events or live browsing-session data.
