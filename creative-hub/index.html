A suggested website structure

  1. Home: explain your business and the next step.
  2. Services: describe your offerings and answer customer questions.
  3. About: explain your approach using verified information.
  4. Contact: agree the appropriate enquiry or booking route.

These are suggestions, not pages already built. Gallery, shop or booking features can be discussed where relevant. This pilot does not invent prices, reviews, qualifications or results.
