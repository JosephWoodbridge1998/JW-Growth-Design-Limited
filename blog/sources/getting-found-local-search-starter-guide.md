# Getting Found: A Local Search Starter Guide

Issue 04 | JW Growth & Design | 25 October 2026

Practical foundations for being discoverable, understandable and contactable.

## Reader outcome
Build an accurate local presence that helps people understand what you offer and how to take the next step - without ranking promises or shortcuts.

## Start here
- Search for your business name and inspect the information shown.
- Check the website link, main category, hours and contact details.
- List the services and genuine locations your website needs to explain.

Google says local results mainly depend on relevance, distance and prominence. You cannot request or pay Google for a better local ranking. [1]

## 01 Manage an accurate Business Profile
If eligible, claim or manage your profile and complete verification. Choose accurate categories, services, opening hours and customer-facing details. Follow Google's address and service-area rules; do not invent premises or add keywords to your business name. [1]

## 02 Make important information agree
Use accurate business and contact details on your site and key listings. Update special opening hours and broken booking links. For service-area businesses, describe the genuine area served without pretending to have an office in every town. [1]

## 03 Create useful service pages
Explain the service, who it suits, what is included and how to enquire. Add genuine location details when useful, such as access, coverage or project examples. Avoid copying the same page and swapping town names. [2,3]

## 04 Make pages understandable and discoverable
Use descriptive page titles and headings, normal crawlable links and useful image descriptions. Check key pages are not accidentally blocked from indexing. Structured data can describe a local business, but does not guarantee an enhanced search result. [2,4]

## 05 Use genuine reviews and relevant proof
Ask for honest customer feedback without manufacturing reviews or incentives. Respond professionally. Keep work photos accurate, current and used with permission. Google notes reviews and links can contribute to prominence. [1]

## 06 Measure useful visibility, not just positions
Use available Business Profile performance, Search Console information and enquiry records to see what happens next. Search visibility varies by query and location. A ranking screenshot is not proof of extra customers or revenue.

## Action plan
### DAY 1 - Audit accuracy
Check your Business Profile and the five online destinations customers use most. Correct factual errors first.

### THIS WEEK - Improve one important service page
Answer real buying questions and link clearly to a suitable contact or booking route. Add specific local detail only where accurate.

### MONTHLY - Review and maintain
Check customer questions, new reviews, useful searches and completed enquiries. Record changes and allow time to observe; do not assume an immediate ranking response.

Illustrative example: a mobile hairdresser explains the areas genuinely covered and links to an appointment request. There is no need to publish a home address as a walk-in salon or create artificial branches to target surrounding towns.
