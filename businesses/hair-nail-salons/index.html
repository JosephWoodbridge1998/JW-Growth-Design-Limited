01 / Do you understand my business?

Your services, clearly presented.

A hairdresser, nail salon or combined studio needs a customer journey shaped around its actual services—not an interchangeable beauty template.

Which service should I choose?

Separate hair and nail categories, with operator-approved descriptions, prices, durations and consultation routes.

Who can help me achieve my look?

Clear team introductions and permissioned work galleries, without invented qualifications or results.

How do I make an appointment?

A clear route to your agreed booking provider, with staff/service compatibility handled by the real setup.

What should I know before visiting?

Bring confirmed opening hours, location, access information and salon-approved consultation or testing policies together.

Can this suit a hair-only or nail-only salon?

Yes. The design and navigation can be scoped around the services you actually provide rather than retaining irrelevant categories.

Can we keep our booking system and domain?

We review access, compatibility and third-party costs before agreeing what can be retained or connected.

Who supplies policies and service guidance?

The real salon supplies and approves consultation, testing, cancellation and suitability information. The concept does not provide clinical advice.

Can we show genuine client work?

Yes, with appropriate image permissions and accurate descriptions. Stock photographs should not be portrayed as your team’s results or false before-and-after pairs.
