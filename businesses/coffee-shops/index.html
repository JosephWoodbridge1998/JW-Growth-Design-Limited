01 / Do you understand my business?

Your shop’s character. The details customers need.

Your website should help someone understand what you serve, where to find you and what a visit could feel like. Whether you focus on takeaway coffee, a place to sit or both, we start with your setup—not an assumption that you need online ordering.

These are potential online friction points we can discuss, rather than assumptions about your business.

“What’s on the menu?”

A menu buried in social posts or available only as an image can make information harder to find.

How JW can helpA readable mobile menu with categories, prices and descriptions supplied and approved by your business.

“Are you open, and how do I get there?”

Opening hours, holiday changes and directions may be scattered across different places.

How JW can helpProminent visiting information, clear directions and a practical process for website updates. Changes to external listings are scoped separately.

“Is this the kind of place I’m looking for?”

A new visitor may want to understand the atmosphere and facilities before deciding to visit.

How JW can helpGenuine shop photography, your story and business-confirmed information about seating, accessibility or other facilities you choose to feature.

“Can I order ahead or ask a question?”

The next step can be unclear when contact and ordering options sit across several platforms.

How JW can helpA clear contact route and, where suitable, a link or connection to your agreed ordering service. Online ordering is optional, not assumed.

03 / What would you build and manage for me?

A website foundation with your coffee shop at its centre.

We agree the pages, features and ongoing support before work begins, keeping your customer journey clear and your service scope understandable.

The website foundation

  • Premium Website Design: a mobile-first design shaped around your coffee shop.
  • On-going Website Delivery: updates and improvements within the scope we agree.
  • Hosting: a managed home for your website.
  • DNS / Custom Domain: domain connection and agreed DNS configuration.
  • HTTPS Enforced: HTTPS configuration for the website.

Options tailored to your coffee shop

  • Readable menus and business-approved product information.
  • Opening hours, holiday notices, location and directions.
  • Shop photography, your story and genuine reviews used with permission.
  • Business-confirmed facilities and visiting information.
  • Contact options or connection to an agreed ordering provider where scoped.

Clear scope before a price is agreed.

Your proposal will separate the initial website build, ongoing service and any third-party costs. Domain registration or renewal, ordering subscriptions and payment-processing charges may be separate; we will identify what applies to your setup.

Support as your shop’s information changes.

We discuss how menu changes, seasonal content and opening-hour updates will be requested and what your ongoing service covers. A website update does not automatically update third-party ordering systems or external business listings.

Practical questions

Do I need online ordering?

No. A website focused on your menu, identity and visiting information may suit your requirements. We can discuss an ordering provider separately if it fits your operations.

Can I keep my domain and existing ordering provider?

We review your current setup first. Keeping your domain and linking or connecting to an existing provider can form part of the agreed scope where access and compatibility allow.

Who supplies menu and allergen information?

Your business supplies and approves that information. We can design how it is presented, but do not invent ingredients, dietary claims, allergen information or facilities.

Can menus and opening hours be updated after launch?

Yes, within the update process and ongoing scope we agree. We also clarify which systems are covered and how changes to external listings or ordering platforms are handled.

How long will the website take?

We agree a timeline after reviewing the scope, content and integrations. You receive the proposed costs and ongoing-service details before committing.
