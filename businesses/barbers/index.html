01 / Do you understand my business?

Built around the way your barbershop works.

Your website should reflect your shop’s character and make practical information easy to find. Whether you take appointments, welcome walk-ins or offer both, we start with your setup rather than forcing you into a standard booking journey.

These are common online friction points we can discuss—not assumptions about your business.

“What does it cost, and what should I book?”

Unclear services or hard-to-find prices can leave visitors without the information they need.

How JW can helpA clear service menu, prices supplied by you, appointment durations where appropriate and an obvious next step.

“Can I book without sending another message?”

Customers may have to move between social profiles, messages and booking links to find their next step.

How JW can helpConnect the website to your agreed booking platform, or provide a straightforward call or enquiry route if that suits your shop better.

“Is this the right barber for me?”

A new visitor may want to see your work and meet the team before choosing a shop.

How JW can helpGenuine work photography, barber introductions and real customer reviews used with permission.

“Where are you, and are you open?”

Essential visiting information can be scattered or out of date.

How JW can helpProminent opening hours, location details, contact options and a clear explanation of your appointments or walk-in policy.

03 / What would you build and manage for me?

Your shop’s identity. A clear website foundation.

We agree the pages, features and ongoing support before work begins. Your website can be tailored without making the underlying service complicated.

The website foundation

  • Premium Website Design: a mobile-first design shaped around your shop.
  • On-going Website Delivery: updates and improvements within the scope we agree.
  • Hosting: a managed home for your website.
  • DNS / Custom Domain: domain connection and agreed DNS configuration.
  • HTTPS Enforced: HTTPS configuration for the website.

Options tailored to your barbershop

  • Services, prices and appointment information.
  • Barber profiles, work galleries and genuine reviews.
  • Opening hours, directions and contact options.
  • Connection to an agreed booking platform.
  • Additional language content or features where scoped.

Clear scope before a price is agreed.

Your proposal will separate the initial website build, ongoing service and any third-party costs. Booking subscriptions, domain registration or renewal, payment processing and SMS charges may be separate; we will identify what applies to your setup.

One point of contact—not a new system to manage alone.

We discuss what you want to keep, what needs changing, how updates will be requested and what the ongoing service covers. No booking increase, search ranking or support-response time is guaranteed by this page.

Practical questions

Can I keep my existing domain and booking system?

We review your current setup first. Keeping your domain and connecting your existing booking platform can form part of the agreed scope where access and compatibility allow.

Do I need professional photographs before we speak?

No. We can discuss the images and written information needed for the website. You should have permission to use any customer photographs or reviews you provide.

Are SMS confirmations included?

Not automatically. Availability and costs depend on your booking provider, plan and the integration agreed. The homepage SMS sequence is simulated.

How long will the website take, and what happens after launch?

We agree a timeline after reviewing the scope, content and integrations. We also define the ongoing service and how changes will be requested before you commit.
