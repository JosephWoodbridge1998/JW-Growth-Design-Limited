01 / Do you understand my business?

Built around the way your barbershop works.

Whether you take appointments, welcome walk-ins or offer both, we start with your setup. These are potential friction points—not assumptions about your shop.

“What does it cost, and what should I book?”

We can structure your service menu, prices and appointment durations so the next step is clear.

“Can I book without another message?”

Connect to an agreed booking platform, or provide a clear call or enquiry route where that suits your business.

“Is this the right barber for me?”

Use genuine work photography, team introductions and real reviews with permission. Matched transformation photos should show the same client and be approved for use.

“Where are you, and are you open?”

Bring opening hours, location, contact options and your confirmed appointments or walk-in policy together.

03 / What would you build and manage for me?

  • Hosting: a managed home for your website.

Options tailored to your barbershop

  • Services, prices, durations and appointment information.

Your proposal separates the initial build, ongoing service and third-party costs. Domain renewals, booking subscriptions, payment processing and messaging may be separate. We agree the timeline, update process and support scope before you commit.

Practical questions

Can I keep my domain and booking system?

We review your current setup first. Retaining the domain and connecting a provider can be scoped where access and compatibility allow.

Can we show real before-and-after cuts?

Yes, with genuine matched photographs and permission to use them. We do not substitute unrelated people or portray edited stock portraits as results.

Are SMS confirmations included?

Not automatically. Provider capabilities, plans, costs and the agreed integration determine availability. Demonstration confirmations do not send messages.

How long will the build take, and what follows launch?

We agree a timeline after reviewing content and integrations, and define ongoing support and the update process before you commit.
