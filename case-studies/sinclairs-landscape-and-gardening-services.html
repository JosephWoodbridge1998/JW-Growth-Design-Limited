Customer Use Case

Sinclair’s Landscape and Gardening Services

From website brief to launch-ready business presence: a branded, mobile-first website designed to make services clear, coverage visible and contact straightforward.

See the live website →

The brief

Sinclair’s needed a clear, practical website for a local landscaping and gardening business. The site needed to explain the services offered, show the areas covered and make it easy for potential customers to get in touch in the way the business prefers.

  • Understand the services: gardening and landscaping services grouped clearly in one place.
  • Check the area covered: named service areas help visitors quickly see whether the business works near them.
  • Get in touch, their way: WhatsApp is the preferred contact route, with email available alongside it.

Project details

Client: Sinclair’s Landscape and Gardening Services
Delivered by: JW Growth & Design
Scope: Basic Package, managed hosting, launch setup and one project-specific animation

What we delivered

Website design & development

A branded, mobile-first website that also works cleanly on tablet, laptop and desktop.

Managed hosting

Hosting and deployment managed by JW Growth & Design within the agreed service scope.

Domain DNS setup

Custom domain connection completed through 123 Reg, giving the business one consistent web address.

HTTPS enforcement

Encrypted browser-to-website access, with HTTPS enforcement configured through JW’s managed hosting and deployment setup.

WhatsApp integration

A direct route into the business’s preferred enquiry channel.

Email integration

An accessible email route for customers who prefer to write.

Before & after gallery

A visual gallery that helps potential customers see examples of completed work.

Custom animation

A project-specific animation that adds character and a polished finishing touch to the website.

Built for the customer journey

The website was designed to make the next step obvious: understand the service, check coverage, then contact the business through the route that suits them. Clear navigation and direct contact routes support the transition from visitor to enquiry.

Post-launch commercial performance has not been measured for this use case. It makes no claims about traffic, enquiries, bookings, search rankings, load times or revenue.
