The brief

Bring the café experience online.

Create a distinctive, mobile-friendly website that communicates the brand’s atmosphere while making the menu, story and visiting information straightforward to explore. Bring design, development and hosting into one delivery conversation, supported by clear technical requirements and commercial responsibilities.

Design delivery

  • A warm visual direction combining expressive typography, a café-inspired colour palette and photography.
  • Clear page hierarchy linking the menu, brand story, coffee-making sequence and visit information.
  • Desktop and mobile layouts shaped around browsing and planning a visit.
  • An original illustrated coffee-making animation and coordinated brand storytelling.

Development delivery

  • A lightweight HTML and CSS website implementation.
  • A categorised menu experience with coffee, tea and chocolate, iced drinks and kitchen sections.
  • Reusable location cards, opening-hour layouts and expandable FAQs.
  • Semantic navigation, a skip link, image descriptions and deferred loading for supporting photography.

Technical foundations

A maintainable route to launch.

  • Keep website source and revisions in GitHub so changes can be reviewed and traced.
  • Confirm the production hosting destination, deployment process and rollback procedure.
  • Verify the client’s domain ownership, DNS configuration and HTTPS before publishing a production address.
  • Check menu controls, navigation, responsive layouts, keyboard access and externally loaded assets.
  • Replace sample locations, opening hours, prices and contact details with business-approved information.
  • Review privacy, tracking and third-party integrations against the final implementation rather than assuming coverage.

These are delivery and acceptance requirements, not claims of completed audits, uptime guarantees or a security certification.

Commercial foundations

Clear scope. Clear responsibilities.

  • Separate the initial design and build from any recurring hosting, maintenance and support services.
  • Record who owns the domain, website source, content and third-party accounts.
  • Agree the approval process for copy, imagery, menu prices and business information.
  • Define update allowances, out-of-scope work and how change requests are priced.
  • Record recurring charges, renewal dates and third-party dependencies in the client agreement.
  • Define handover, access transfer and the process for ending or moving the hosting service.

No package price, support response time, included maintenance allowance or contractual service level is published without confirmed terms.

What this supports commercially

The website brings the café’s identity and practical customer information into one place. A clear menu structure helps visitors explore the offer; the story and photography communicate atmosphere; location and contact sections provide a route towards planning a visit once verified business details are supplied.

These are the intended functions of the design, not measured increases in visits, enquiries or revenue. No client testimonial or business-performance result is claimed.
